About Me

I am a distinguished Professor of Computer Science at Aligarh Muslim University with over thirty-seven years of experience in teaching, research, and academic administration.

I have the distinction of being the first person to complete Ph.D. in the Department of Computer Science at AMU, Aligarh in 2006. I also hold a D.Sc. in Computer Science (Crown University International, 2023). My research focuses on Software Reliability, Security and Cryptography, Software Engineering, E-Learning, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence.

I have visited various countries including Australia, France, Canada, and South Africa for research and acquiring academic excellence. I also served as Associate Professor and Director of Studies at the Australian Institute of Engineering & Technology, Melbourne. Currently serving as Member of Board of Governor (BoG) at the University of Science and Technology Meghalaya (USTM) since October 2022.
